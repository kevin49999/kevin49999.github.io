## Black Coffee

<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/4992427" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://kevin499.itch.io/black-coffee">Black Coffee by kevin499</a></iframe>

A coffee shop simulation game inspired by [Overcooked](https://store.steampowered.com/app/448510/Overcooked/).

I did the programming and created the pixel art with [Pixaki](https://pixaki.com/). 

Made with [Godot](https://godotengine.org/).
